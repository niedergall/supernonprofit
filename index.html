SUPER NONPROFIT!

Run a tiny publishing house. Everyone does everything. Nothing is on fire (yet).
Move: WASD / arrow keys   Action: E, Space, Enter or mouse click (hold it to work)   M mute   P pause
Counters: set things down on any counter (the wooden tables) and pick them up later. Hand-off: stand next to a teammate with empty hands and press Action to give them what you're carrying.
Bots: computer teammates take on whatever needs doing (phones, packing, forklift, mowing...). Pick 0 to 3 above. Large orders (5-6 books) show up now and then.
Large orders: take a LARGE box from its own dispenser (small boxes for small orders). A packed large order becomes a heavy crate - two players press Action to lift it together, carry it to the right platform (you stay tethered, so walk as a team) and press Action to ship. Press Action away from a platform to set it down.
Tight spaces: you and your teammates bump and shove each other, doors swing open when pushed (and bash anyone on the other side), and the gap between the warehouse racks only fits one person at a time.
On a phone or tablet: use the on-screen joystick and buttons (landscape works best).
The loop: Customer Service answers phones and web orders → Warehouse grabs books, packs a box, ships it to the right platform (or rides it across the warehouse on the conveyor - pick it up at the other end and place it yourself). Editorial turns manuscripts into books, a truck brings them, and the forklift unloads them. IT fixes crashed computers. Maintenance mows the lawn. Sales cold-calls and sends reps to conventions.

Jobs

📞 Customer ServiceAnswer phones and web orders, send them to the warehouse.
📦 WarehouseGrab books, pack a box, ship it to the right platform. Unload trucks with the forklift and recycle the packaging. Ordered boxes arrive by truck too.
🔧 ITComputers crash. Grab a toolkit and fix them.
🚐 SalesLoad the convention kit on the van. The rep brings back orders.
🌿 MaintenanceMow the lawn before the neighbors complain. Rain slows the mower.
